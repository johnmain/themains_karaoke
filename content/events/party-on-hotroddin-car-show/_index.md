## Party On Hotroddin Car Show — July 26, 2026

Maintec Entertainment is proud to provide professional **DJ and MC services** for the **Party On Hotroddin Car Show** in Ethelbert, Manitoba on **Sunday, July 26th, 2026**.

![Party On Hotroddin Car Show in Ethelbert, Manitoba — classic cars on display at the outdoor grounds with professional DJ entertainment by Maintec Entertainment in the Parkland Region](/images/party-on-hotroddin-car-show.jpg)

### Event Details

- **📅 Date:** Sunday, July 26th, 2026
- **📍 Location:** Ethelbert, Manitoba
- **🎵 Entertainment:** Professional DJ & MC by Maintec Entertainment

Whether you're a car enthusiast or just looking for a great community event on a beautiful summer Sunday, this is the perfect way to kick off the season.
