Hosting a Karaoke Night?

Ready to bring the house down? Here are some quick tips to ensure your karaoke event is a legendary success:

  • Set the Stage: Make sure the lyrics are visible to everyone, not just the singer.
  • Mix it Up: Alternate between upbeat bangers and power ballads to keep the energy dynamic.
  • The Golden Rule: Cheering is mandatory! Support every performer, regardless of pitch or key.
